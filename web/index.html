Temperature

Red Wigglers (Eisenia fetida): ideal 65°F – 77°F (tolerates 39°F – 90°F).

  • Ideal is 65–77°F. The chart shades this band while Thresholds is on.
  • Tolerable is outside that ideal, at 50–65°F or 77–85°F.
  • Stress is cold stress below 50°F, or heat stress above 85°F. Thresholds draws dashed lines at 50°F and 85°F. Turn it off to fit the chart to the probe readings.
  • Outside tolerance is below 39°F or above 90°F.
  • Freezing is 32°F or below. A frozen bin can be fatal.

Broader vermiculture limits: ideal 60–77°F (15–25°C), survival 40–90°F (4–32°C). A bin with no reading for 30 minutes is marked missing.

Soil moisture

Percent is 0 in dry air and 100 at the wet limit set in the sketch. Raw is the unscaled analog reading. The chart, minimum, and maximum use the selected time range.
